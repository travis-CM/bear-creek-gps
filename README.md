# GolfHub (Bear Creek GPS)

Personal golf GPS and scramble scorer for Bear Creek Golf Club (East and West courses), DFW Airport, TX.

Open: https://travis-cm.github.io/bear-creek-gps/

- Live front / middle (pin) / back yardages from phone GPS, tap-anywhere distances
- "Plays like": USGS 1 m lidar slope + live wind, temperature, humidity and pressure (Open-Meteo)
- Bunker and water reach / carry numbers
- Scramble mode: 4 golfers, whose shot was used, gimmicks (mulligan, string, ...), voice dictation
- Pin sheet photo + per-hole pin placement, pace of play, auto scorecard with ± override

Data: hole/green/bunker shapes © OpenStreetMap contributors (refreshed by the "Fetch course map data" workflow), imagery Esri World Imagery, elevation USGS 3DEP, weather Open-Meteo, scorecard GolfPass.
